# GROUP-1-NRC-REPO
UniGrid is a centralized, AI-powered campus platform designed to unify university learning, peer mentorship, and cross-disciplinary student innovation into a secure digital ecosystem. By utilizing official university single sign-on, students are automatically routed into their specific departments using their unique academic Program Code. Inside these program hubs, a vertical mentorship network safely connects 1st-year through 4th-year students to solve complex academic problems together. To ensure absolute accessibility, the platform uses data-efficient WebSockets and WebRTC for entirely cost-free, in-app text and voice communication over campus Wi-Fi. It features a structured, course-coded library where students can upload and download crowdsourced lecture notes, past exam papers, and current timetables. This repository is enhanced by a smart search engine that automatically embeds relevant YouTube educational videos alongside text materials to assist visual learners. Going a step further, the platform integrates a local Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) AI Engine that acts as an instant 24/7 tutor. This AI reads uploaded course documents to explain complex concepts in simple terms and automatically generates custom mock quizzes or flashcards. Beyond individual studying,UniGrid actively breaks down departmental silos through a dedicated module called The Innovation Forge. This space allows students from completely different disciplines, such as Physics and Computer Science, to merge their technical skill sets and co-create advanced, multi-disciplinary projects. Profiles are tagged with specific micro-credentials, allowing the platform's AI to automatically recommend the ideal campus partners to complete project teams. Finally, the platform features a built-in Hackathon Arena with team formation tools and live leaderboards to showcase student talent directly to corporate sponsors. Ultimately, UniGrid elevates academic performance, reduces student data costs, and transforms the university into a thriving incubator for real-world student startups.
